1. Scenario 1: A gated carpark with a fixed number of spaces. The gate has to know when to open and when not to.

This system will be used by residents and visitors when they need to park. Residents will have a key card that they need to scan on the scanner on the gate which opens the gate. There will be a pressure plate on the floor so after the car rolls over it it will start a few seconds timer to close the gate to make sure the car gets in. Once in the parking area each car goes into a parking spot with pressure plates which allows the system to know a car is there. There will also be a screen at the gate which tells you how many spots remain. If there are no spots remaining the gate will not open even with a key card. The car will have to now wait till a parking spot opens and when it does the gate will then open (you would have had to use your key card).

Peer feedback
When a car leaves, the gate should automatically open without a key card because it knows an exit from an entry.

There should also be a clock on the system that lets it know if and when a curfew is set and no cars are supposed to come in or go out except through an override, even when there are still spaces.
